How to play

Shape the quarry
Pick a material, then drag or tap on the quarry. Copper and sand fall, basalt forms walls, and the eraser cuts through terrain.
Break pixels
Press B or choose Break to chip terrain, trees, buildings, creatures, and bosses into falling debris. Use a 1 px brush for single pixels.
Change the sky
Pick Day or Night to hold the sky, or Auto for a moving sun and moon. Rainfall has its own Off, Rain, and Storm controls.
Place a Tri Portal
Choose Tri Portal under Scenery and place it on dry ground. It opens into a mouth and releases Tri Blobs at night. Use Eraser or Break to remove it.
Place Tri Opus
Open Tri variants, choose Creatures, and select Tri Opus. Click or tap the quarry to spawn one.
Explore Mosswake
Use the map arrows, drag the map slider, or press A and D to move the view across all five areas.
Add life
Place copperbacks or Mosswake ducks, or plant Tri on dry ground. When Tri touches a pool, it turns the water into thick, flesh-colored ichor. Erase the clump to remove its growth and clear the pool.
Choose a variant
Switch between Normal and Tri variants above Materials, Creatures, and Scenery. Place birches, huts, pumps, fences, or docks in either palette. Growing Tri converts normal scenery it reaches and produces Tri Blobs, Tri Lings, and floating Tri Brains.
Summon bosses
Place the Copper Giant on dry ground, the Lake’s Corrupt Terror over water or ichor, and the Eye of the Sky in open air. A Tri clump can summon an Eye of the Sky after four minutes of active play.
Play with the keyboard
Press 1–9 to choose a tool, E for eraser, and Space to pause. With the quarry focused, use the arrow keys to aim and Enter to place.
Cross Mosswake
Use A/D, the arrow keys, or tap the ground. Use the map buttons to walk to either edge portal or back to the tower. Walk over copper coins to collect them.
Prepare for the raid
Build, repair, or reinforce a wall at its frame. Press E or use the action button at a camp to hire guards for 3 copper each, with no limit on the roster. Guards fill the two banks evenly. After hiring three guards, either keep recruiting or train a guard as a veteran for 4 copper. Recruits take shelter until nearby Tri Opus pass.
Bridge market
Build both forward walls for 5 copper each to open the market east of the tower. Approach a stall and press E to shop. Buying a weapon equips it. Face a Tri and press F or use Swing to attack.
Keep the light burning
At dusk, Tri Opus walk out of both portals and strike your defenses with pulses of light. Each dawn starts a new day until the tower falls.
Blood moons
Every tenth night has a blood moon. An Ichor Sprayer appears at the west portal on night 20 and every night from 30 onward, releasing new Tri Opus in bursts of ichor.
Daytime patrols
Guards patrol the banks and hunt pigeons. Walk over dropped copper to collect it. Guards return to their posts at dusk.
Ring the dusk bell
Use the tower in the middle of the bridge to start a raid early. Repair it there during the day if it took damage.
Save the watch
Open Save slots to create a named save or update one. Load a slot to return to the day or raid you saved.

Clear the quarry?

This removes the terrain, materials, creatures, bosses, scenery, and Tri patches and turns off the rain.
