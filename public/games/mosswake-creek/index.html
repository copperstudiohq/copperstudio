How to play

Shape the quarry
Pick a material, then drag or tap on the quarry. Copper and sand fall, basalt forms walls, and the eraser cuts through terrain.
Break pixels
Press B or choose Break to chip terrain, trees, buildings, creatures, and bosses into falling debris. Use a 1 px brush for single pixels.
Change the sky
Pick Day or Night to hold the sky, or Auto for a moving sun and moon. Rainfall has its own Off, Rain, and Storm controls.
Place a Tri Portal
Choose Tri Portal under Scenery and place it on dry ground. It opens into a mouth and releases Tri Blobs at night. Use Eraser or Break to remove it.
Place Tri Opus
Open Tri variants, choose Creatures, and select Tri Opus. Click or tap the quarry to spawn one.
Explore Mosswake
Use the map arrows, drag the map slider, or press A and D to move the view across all five areas.
Add life
Place copperbacks or Mosswake ducks, or plant Tri on dry ground. When Tri touches a pool, it turns the water into thick, flesh-colored ichor. Erase the clump to remove its growth and clear the pool.
Choose a variant
Switch between Normal and Tri variants above Materials, Creatures, and Scenery. Place birches, huts, rows of connected houses, fences, or docks in either palette. Growing Tri converts normal scenery it reaches and produces Tri Blobs, Tri Lings, and floating Tri Brains.
Summon bosses
Place the Copper Giant on dry ground, the Lake’s Corrupt Terror over water or ichor, and the Eye of the Sky in open air. A Tri clump can summon an Eye of the Sky after four minutes of active play.
Play with the keyboard
Press 1–9 to choose a tool, E for eraser, and Space to pause. With the quarry focused, use the arrow keys to aim and Enter to place.
Cross Mosswake
Use A/D, the arrow keys, or tap the ground. Use the map buttons to walk to either edge portal or back to the blue signal. Walk over copper coins to collect them.
Prepare for the raid
Build, repair, or reinforce a wall at its frame. Press E or use the action button at a camp to hire guards for 3 copper each, with no limit on the roster. Guards fill the two banks evenly. After hiring three guards, either keep recruiting or train a guard as a veteran for 4 copper. Recruits take shelter until nearby Tri Opus pass.
Bridge market
Build both forward walls for 5 copper each to open the market east of the tower. Approach a stall and press E to shop. Buying a weapon equips it. Face a Tri and press F or use Swing to attack.
Chop and sell wood
Stand beside a tall birch and press E or Chop birch three times. The tree falls and gives you wood. Either bridge market vendor pays 2 copper per wood.
Command your builders
Hire builders at open tents for 3 copper each, then type “fix walls” in Builder orders or choose Repair defenses. They keep repairing damaged defenses each day until you order “stop repairs.” Each wall or wall tower repair costs 2 copper, or 1 with the Repair Kit; the signal tower costs 3. You pay once when the first health point is restored, then the builder finishes that repair at 1 health every 2 seconds. Interrupted repairs keep their payment, including after saving. Without enough copper, builders wait and resume when you have enough. Builders still return before night. Use Upgrade walls to order automatic upgrades too.
Automatic wall upgrades
Type “upgrade walls” or “auto upgrade,” or choose Upgrade walls in Builder orders. Builders repair damaged defenses first, then improve existing walls: reinforce for 5 copper, then build a wall tower for 200. Each upgrade needs four seconds of work at the wall, and its full price is taken when it finishes. They wait for enough coins, return before night, and keep the order across days and saves. Use Stop work or type “stop upgrades” to cancel. Build empty wall sites yourself.
Wall towers · 200 copper
Build and reinforce a palisade, then repair it to full health. At that wall, press E or choose Build wall tower to upgrade it for 200 copper. Each of the four walls can become a tower with 30 health. Two existing guards use the elevated platform at their outermost standing defense; they still gather coins by day and return for the raid. Damaged or destroyed wall towers can be repaired without buying the upgrade again. Named saves keep the towers.
Arcing arrows
Soldiers shoot arrows up into the air. Gravity curves their flight, and they deal damage only when they physically strike a creature. Moving targets can dodge them. Veterans aim more tightly than regular soldiers; long shots and flying targets are harder. Stray arrows fall and briefly stick in the ground. Pigeon hunting also needs a real hit, and named saves keep arrows already in flight.
From campfire to tower
During the day, stand beside the blue campfire to build a signal tent for 7 copper. The tent has a blue torch and a chair. Upgrade it to a tower for 100 copper, then enter the dining room for free. Approach a dining chair and press E to sit. Press E or move to stand. The outside watch pauses while you are indoors. Named saves keep your signal upgrade, room position and seated chair.
Keep the light burning
At dusk, Tri Opus strike your defenses. Close strikes and Flyer dives can hurt you and knock copper loose. Walk over it to recover it. You heal at dawn. The watch ends if you fall or the signal goes out.
Blood moons
Every tenth night has a blood moon. Ichor Sprayers appear at both portals on night 20 and every night from 30 onward, releasing new Tri Opus in bursts of ichor.
Tri Flyers · night 40 onward
Small, hunched pink Tri crawl on four clawed wing-arms, then spread those same limbs to fly from both sides. Flying bats try to land on your soldiers and infect them. Infected soldiers turn pink and hunched, leave your guard roster, and attack your defenses like Tri Opus. Shoot the flyers before they land. Named saves preserve the new enemies and infection.
Daytime patrols
Guards gather copper across the whole map, carry it to you, and drop it at your feet. The pile adds to your total when you pick it up; guards leave delivered piles for you. If delivery would leave too little time to get home before dusk, they keep the copper and bring it the next day. You can still walk over other coins to collect them yourself.
Ring the dusk bell
Use the signal in the middle of the bridge to call the guards home early. The portals open once everyone is at their defenses. Repair the signal there during the day if it took damage.
Save the watch
Open Save slots to create a named save or update one. Load a slot to return to the day or raid you saved.

Clear the quarry?

This removes the terrain, materials, creatures, bosses, scenery, and Tri patches and turns off the rain.
